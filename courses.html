TRAINING PROGRAMS

Our Courses

Forces Academy Faisalabad offers a wide range of training programs designed to help candidates achieve excellence at every stage of their preparation. Our experienced ex-forces instructors, proven ISSB techniques, and candidate-focused approach prepare learners for selection boards, physical tests, and competitive entry examinations.

Cadet College Entry Test

Comprehensive academic and physical preparation for Cadet College entrance examinations, covering all required subjects and test formats.

  • Duration: 6 Months
  • Eligibility: Grade 8 Pass
Learn More

PMA Long Course

Complete preparation for Pakistan Military Academy selection, including academic test, interview, and physical fitness standards.

  • Duration: 6–12 Months
  • Eligibility: Intermediate / Bachelor's
Learn More

ISSB Preparation

Build a strong foundation for the Inter Services Selection Board, covering intelligence tests, psychological assessment, and outdoor tasks.

  • Duration: 3 Months
  • Eligibility: Passed Initial Test
Learn More

Pakistan Navy Prep

Strengthen academic knowledge and test-taking skills for Pakistan Navy entry tests through focused coaching and mock assessments.

  • Duration: 6 Months
  • Eligibility: Matric / Intermediate
Learn More

Pakistan Air Force Prep

Specialized coaching for PAF initial tests, aptitude assessment, and interview preparation for aspiring officers.

  • Duration: 6 Months
  • Eligibility: Intermediate (Pre-Engineering)
Learn More

Physical Fitness Training

Structured physical training program to build strength, stamina, and endurance for Armed Forces fitness standards and selection tests.

  • Duration: 3–6 Months
  • Eligibility: Open to All Candidates
Learn More

Lady Cadet Course (LCC)

Exclusive training program tailored for female candidates preparing for direct commission as officers in the Pakistan Army across various corps.

  • Duration: 3–6 Months
  • Eligibility: Master's / BS (4 Years)
Learn More

Short Service Commission (SSC)

Intensive preparation for professional degree holders (Engineers, IT, Medical) aspiring for direct officer entry in Army, Navy, and PAF.

  • Duration: 3 Months
  • Eligibility: Relevant BS / BE / Professional Degree
Learn More

Armed Forces Nursing Services (AFNS)

Dedicated academic and interview coaching for female candidates seeking commissioned rank in military nursing services.

  • Duration: 3–6 Months
  • Eligibility: F.Sc Pre-Medical
Learn More

Initial Intelligence & Academic Test

Rigorous practice for Verbal, Non-Verbal Intelligence, and Academic subjects to ensure 100% success in the initial computer-based test.

  • Duration: 2 Months
  • Eligibility: Matric / Intermediate
Learn More

GDP & CAE (Air Force)

High-intensity preparation for General Duty Pilot and College of Aeronautical Engineering entry tests including specialized physics and math modules.

  • Duration: 4 Months
  • Eligibility: F.Sc Pre-Engineering / ICS
Learn More

Soldier / Sailor / Airman Recruitment

Complete non-commissioned cadre entry prep focusing on written test syllabus, physical efficiency tests, and medical screening guidance.

  • Duration: 3 Months
  • Eligibility: Matric / Middle
Learn More
