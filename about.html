ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

Shaping Future Officers Through Disciplined Mentorship

Forces Academy Faisalabad was established with a singular mission: to cultivate the next generation of officers for the Pakistan Armed Forces. We provide an end-to-end coaching framework that balances rigorous academic mastery, psychological readiness, and outdoor physical conditioning.

Our faculty comprises seasoned ex-military instructors who bring real-world evaluation insight to everyday lessons. By focusing on Officer Like Qualities (OLQs), analytical thinking, and character building, we ensure every student leaves fully prepared to excel at ISSB and initial selection hurdles.

Mission & Vision

Our purpose is to prepare candidates for the Armed Forces while developing the discipline, physical fitness, and confidence needed for a successful military career.

Our Mission

To provide rigorous, high-quality training that builds discipline, physical fitness, critical thinking, and personal character. We are committed to preparing candidates for ISSB, PMA, Naval Academy, and Air Force selection through proven methods and dedicated instructors.

Our Vision

To become the leading forces preparatory academy by nurturing confident, disciplined, and capable leaders who are prepared to serve their nation with honour and excel in their military careers.

Why Choose Forces Academy?

We provide a disciplined training environment where candidates are guided to build physical fitness, confidence, and the skills needed to succeed in Armed Forces selection.
